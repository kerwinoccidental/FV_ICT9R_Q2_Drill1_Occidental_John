Beginner's Guide for Volleyball




📝 Basic Rules of Volleyball

  • A ball hitting a boundary line is in.
  • A ball hitting outside the boundary line is out.
  • Three touch rule in returning the ball to the opposing team.
  • A rotation is needed and should always be observed.

🏐 Volleyball Equipments

  • Volleyball, the basic equipment when playing volleyball.
  • Knee Pads, in case if you are diving a lot.
  • Proper shoes, to avoid slipping during the game.
  • Court, the common location where volleyball games happen.

💡 Basic Techniques

  • Underhand Serve, the most basic serve to use for beginners.
  • Overhand Serve, prvides more control in your serving.
  • Positioning, an essential thing to do to be prepared in receiving the ball.
  • Great Communication, the most important element in a team.
